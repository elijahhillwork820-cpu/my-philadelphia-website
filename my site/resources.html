Helpful Philadelphia Resources

These resources provide information about Philadelphia's history, landmarks, art, and role in American history.

  • Independence Hall

    Visit the National Park Service Independence Hall page

    Independence Hall is an important Philadelphia landmark where the Declaration of Independence and the United States Constitution were debated and signed.

    Why it is useful: Learn about Philadelphia's important role in American history.

  • Liberty Bell

    Visit the National Park Service Liberty Bell page

    The Liberty Bell is one of Philadelphia's most recognizable historic landmarks. The National Park Service provides information about the bell and the Liberty Bell Center.

    Why it is useful: Learn about one of Philadelphia's best-known symbols and its historical significance.

  • Philadelphia City Government

    Visit the Official City of Philadelphia Website

    The official City of Philadelphia website provides information about city government, public services, programs, departments, and community resources.

    Why it is useful: Find official information about Philadelphia and services available to residents.

  • Philadelphia Museum of Art

    Visit the Philadelphia Museum of Art

    The Philadelphia Museum of Art features art collections, exhibitions, tours, educational programs, and the famous Rocky Steps.

    Why it is useful: Explore Philadelphia's art, culture, architecture, and landmarks.

  • National Constitution Center

    Visit the National Constitution Center

    The National Constitution Center provides exhibits, educational programs, and resources about the United States Constitution and America's founding.

    Why it is useful: Learn about Philadelphia's connection to the founding of the United States and the Constitution.
